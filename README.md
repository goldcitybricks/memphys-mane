# The Legend of MEMPHYS Mane

A retro 90s beat-'em-up (Turtles in Time feel) set in Memphis, TN, starring MEMPHYS Mane. A free promo game for MEMPHYS Water, built to run in a phone browser and embed on memphyswater.com.

Playable now: **Mission 1, "The Sip"**, in the alley behind Uncle T's Pit at Park & Airways, Orange Mound, at night, **Mission 2, "Old Memphis"**, in Memphis, Egypt, 3,000 years ago, **Mission 3, "Fight Night"**, a 1-on-1 boxing match against Pip at Mid-South Coliseum, **Mission 4, "Bridge Run"**, a Mario Kart style chase across the Hernando-DeSoto Bridge in Big Blue, **Mission 5, "The Plant"**, a Chex Quest style first-person run through the DeNile Water plant on Presidents Island, **Mission 6, "Beale Below"**, a brawler through the flooded tunnels under Beale Street, **Mission 7, "Crowe Live"**, a 1-on-1 fight against Victor Crowe's robot body double on the riverfront stage at Tom Lee Park, **Mission 8, "The Loop"**, three laps around I-240 in Big Blue while GigaZ makes Memphis forget, **Mission 9, "The Gigantum"**, a first-person run through AetherCorp's data center to get Uncle T's memory back, and **Mission 10, "The Source"**, the finale: Victor Crowe in person, then the Pharaoh of DeNile, then the end credits. That is the whole story.

## Play

- **Phone:** turn it sideways. Left thumb anywhere on the left half is the stick. HIT, JUMP, SIP, FLEX on the right. Tap to advance dialogue, SKIP to jump past the intro.
- **Keyboard:** arrows/WASD move, Z or J punch, X/K/Space jump, C or L sip, V or I flex, Esc or P pause.
- **Fight Night (Mission 3), phone:** BLOCK (hold) and DODGE on the left; JAB, HOOK, FLEX and SUPER on the right. Tap anywhere to get up when you're down.
- **Crowe Live (Mission 7)** uses the same fight controls.
- **Fight Night, keyboard:** Z/J/→ jab, X/K hook, hold C/L/↓ block, Space/Shift/← dodge, V/I flex (grill shine), B/U/↑ super.
- **Bridge Run (Mission 4), phone:** Big Blue drives herself. Left thumb steers. ITEM, DRIFT (hold) and SIP on the right. Hold DRIFT with no turn to brake.
- **Bridge Run, keyboard:** left/right or A/D steer, Z/J/Space item, hold X/K/Shift/↓ to drift (or brake), C/L/↑ sip, Esc or P pause.
- **The Loop (Mission 8)** uses the same driving controls.
- **The Gigantum (Mission 9)** uses the same controls as The Plant.
- **The Source (Mission 10)** starts with the boxing controls from Fight Night (Victor), then switches to the brawler controls from The Sip (the Pharaoh). In the finale, tap anywhere (or press any button) on the beat.
- **The Plant (Mission 5), phone:** left thumb walks (up/down) and turns (left/right); drag the right side of the screen to look. Hold SPRAY, tap SIP and DOLLA.
- **The Plant, keyboard:** W/S or up/down walk, A/D or left/right turn, Q/E step sideways, hold Z/J/Space to spray, C/L sip, V/I "dolla", click and drag to look, Esc or P pause.
- Add `?play` to the URL to skip the title screen, or `?play=2` through `?play=10` to jump straight into that mission. Beating Mission 1 unlocks a mission picker on the title screen, and the results screen has NEXT.

## Develop

```bash
npm install
npm run dev        # http://localhost:5173
npm run build      # static site in dist/
npm run assets     # regenerate all art + audio (needs ffmpeg)
```

Pushing to `main` (or a `claude/*` branch) builds and deploys to GitHub Pages via `.github/workflows/pages.yml`, once Pages is turned on in the repo settings with Source set to "GitHub Actions". The build is a plain static folder with relative paths, so it can also be dropped into any folder on memphyswater.com.

## How it's put together

```
src/
  main.ts                    game config: 216px tall, 384 to 480 wide, scales up crisp
  core/                      shared by every mission and mode
    assets.ts                loads every sprite sheet from public/assets/art/art.json
    controls.ts              one input model for keyboard + touch, with input buffering
    audio.ts, save.ts        music/SFX/mute, local best scores
    modes.ts                 the mission list and the play modes (brawler, fight and driving now; shooter and hub later)
  modes/brawler/             the reusable Turtles-in-Time engine
    BrawlerScene.ts          camera locks, waves, hit detection, hitstop, cars, pickups, speech bubbles, script helpers
    Mane.ts                  the player: movement, combo, jump kick, Sip/Mane Mode, flex, knockdowns
    Shiesty.ts               enemy AI (rushers and throwers, two attack tokens at a time); each mission skins it
    JackalCaptain.ts         Mission 2 boss
    Driller.ts               Mission 6 boss: drill jab, charge and overheat, floor slam with shockwave and falling rocks, crew calls
    Props.ts                 cars, pickups, trash cans, bottles
  modes/fight/               the reusable 1-on-1 fight engine (Mortal Kombat / Fight Night style, side view)
    FightScene.ts            rounds, the clock, hit resolution (block, dodge, counters), knockdowns and the ref's count, the super, cutscene helpers
    Fighter.ts               a fighter: state machine plus a data-driven move list (windup tells, damage, chip, armor, unblockable)
    BoxerMane.ts             Mane's moves and controls
    Pip.ts                   Mission 3 opponent: move list, per-round styles, the AI
    CrowePrime.ts            Mission 7 opponent: jab, selfie-stick swing, ping projectile, rocket dash (dodge it and it crashes), the download beam, and the prediction that reads a punch you repeat
    FightUI.ts               fight HUD, touch buttons, the count, pause and continue
  modes/driving/             the reusable racing engine (Mario Kart / arcade racer style, pseudo-3D from behind the car)
    Track.ts                 the course: segments with curves, hills, themes (city, ramp, bridge), arch spans and roadside props; point-to-point or laps
    DriveScene.ts            projection and drawing, the car (auto-gas, steering, drift boosts, Mane Mode), traffic and AetherCar AI, items, hazards, the boss vehicle, laps on a looping track, allies that hold formation and plow, continues, script helpers, a live engine sound
    DriveUI.ts               driving HUD (cans, item slot, speed, TN-to-AR progress or the lap, the tanker's health or a mission meter), steering stick and buttons, fact card, pause and continue
  modes/shooter/             the reusable first-person engine (Chex Quest style raycaster drawn pixel by pixel into a canvas)
    ShooterScene.ts          map cells (walls, sliding doors, belts, spills), textured walls/floors/ceilings with banded light, billboard sprites, solid props, Drifter, Pip and drone AI, shootable targets, the Hydrator with a little aim help, Mane Mode, the dolla, getting capped (or frozen), continues, script helpers; a mission can swap the wall and floor sheets, fog and Drifter looks
    ShooterUI.ts             HUD (health, cans, goal, boss bar, an extra mission meter), move stick + drag-to-look + buttons, the capped or frozen screen, the fact card, pause and continue
  missions/
    Mission1.ts              The Sip: level layout, the intro, waves, the ending
    Mission2.ts              Old Memphis: the market, the grill gag, Nia's memory, the Jackal Captain, the pull-back
    Mission3.ts              Fight Night: the entrance, the corners, the round cards, the KO photo, "recycled!", O.G.'s tip
    Mission4.ts              Bridge Run: the tire shop and the phone book, the course and who's on it, the tanker chase, the glyph and the geyser
    Mission5.ts              The Plant: Presidents Island, the Hydrator, the map and who's in it, the Pip army, the Foreman, the drains
    Mission6.ts              Beale Below: Beale Street, the ghost murals and their music, the drill crews, the clay, the Driller, the golden jar and Uncle T
    Mission7.ts              Crowe Live: the launch concert, the livestream and its chat on the LED screen, Victor's yacht cam, GigaZ taking over, "clip that", the face peeling off
    Mission8.ts              The Loop: Uncle T's Pit forgetting, the I-240 course, the gold and blue gantries, the memory meter and the signs that forget, O.G.'s convoy and the wedge, the Gigantum exit
    Mission9.ts              The Gigantum: the gate, the intake, the server floor and its memory banks, cold storage and the frozen core, Uncle T's upload bar, the flood, the fall into the aquifer
    Mission10.ts             The Source, part 1: down through the layers, Victor Crowe in rocket boots (fake pause, refill, rocket dash), the jar, the fusion
    Mission10B.ts            The Source, part 2: the Pip army, the Pharaoh of DeNile, the drain, "Memphis, remember!", the beat super, the crown, the pour, the morning after
    lines.ts                 Mane's lines and enemy trash talk, in one place for review
  ui/UIScene.ts              HUD, touch controls, pause and continue screens
  scenes/                    Boot, Title, Results, Finale (end credits and the post-credits scene), credits
tools/
  art/                       first-pass pixel art, drawn in code to the story bible's specs
  audio/                     tiny synth that makes all the music and sound effects
```

Later missions plug in as new scenes: another brawler extends `BrawlerScene` and writes a script; another 1-on-1 boss (Victor's phase 1 in Mission 10) extends `FightScene` with a new `Fighter` subclass and move list, the way Crowe Prime does in Mission 7; Mission 9 (The Gigantum) extends `ShooterScene` with its own wall and floor sheets and boss, and the open-world hub reuses the brawler's side-scrolling camera and art. Leaderboards, the Join the Keepers email save and promo codes have stub hooks in `core/modes.ts` and `core/save.ts`.

### Swapping in final art

Every sprite is a PNG in `public/assets/art/` with frames side by side, facing right, feet on the same baseline (the specs in the story bible). Replace a file with one of the same frame size and frame order and the game picks it up; frame ranges per animation are listed in `public/assets/art/art.json`. Music and sound effects swap the same way in `public/assets/audio/`.
