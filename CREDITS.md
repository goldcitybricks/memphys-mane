## Places and names

Uncle T's Pit is fictional. Its look is based on the building at Park & Airways in Orange Mound, with fictional names on every sign. No real people, teams, mascots or brand logos appear in the game other than MEMPHYS. The drive-out cars are generic sedans with no badges. Old Memphis in Mission 2 is an imagined version of ancient Memphis, Egypt; its temple paintings are decorative patterns, not real hieroglyphic writing. Mid-South Coliseum in Mission 3 is a real Memphis landmark used as a backdrop; DeNile Water, Pip, AetherCorp and the fight night are fictional, and the fan signs and banners use made-up names only. The Hernando-DeSoto Bridge, the Pyramid and downtown Memphis in Mission 4 are real landmarks drawn as backdrops (the Pyramid carries no signs); Big Blue, the drive-out sedans and the AetherCars are generic shapes with no badges or logos, and the billboards advertise only MEMPHYS and the game's fictional DeNile and AetherCorp.
